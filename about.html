About the project

Making student accommodation easier to navigate.

Campus Connect Living is a student accommodation platform concept focused on students who need a practical way to explore housing in Pretoria.

Our story

Campus Connect Living was inspired by the difficulties students can experience when moving away from home to continue their education. Finding affordable accommodation can be challenging, especially when students are unfamiliar with the area.

The concept brings accommodation information into one place so students can compare locations, prices, room types and facilities before making decisions.

Our mission

To connect students with safe, affordable and reliable accommodation while making the search process simple, clear and less stressful.

Our vision

To become a trusted student accommodation platform that supports students throughout their academic journey.

Who we help

  • College and university students
  • First-year students
  • Students relocating from other provinces
  • International students
  • Parents looking for accommodation for their children

Student safety

Accommodation scams can cause serious financial harm. Students should inspect properties where possible, verify the accommodation provider, read rental agreements carefully and be cautious about requests for deposits or payments before key details have been confirmed.
